        1. 0Each session

          • Start Docker Desktop, then open Lab Tool. It syncs Intigriti at start, then every 20 minutes.

          • r

            Sync now.

          • c

            Changes since the last sync: new programs, scope, bounties, rules. Only there when something changed.

        2. 1Pick a program

          • jk

            Move through the ranking.

          • ↵o

            Open the program on Intigriti and read its rules.

          • a

            On a program you have to join: application state, none → applied → accepted → rejected.

          • host/bounty program show <handle>

            Scope, rules, recon, findings and hours on one screen. Leave out the handle for the active program.

        3. 2Go live

          • u

            Load the selected program into the scope guard, starting it if needed. One program at a time: switching from another asks for a second u. All traffic goes through the guard.

          • T

            Start or stop the timer for the active program. Its hours drive €/h.

          • g

            Stop the guard when you’re done.

        4. 3Recon

          • l

            Look here: hosts from passive recon (crt.sh and DNS), most interesting first, new ones highlighted.

          • m

            Map the active program with httpx and katana at its rate. m again stops; a later m resumes and skips hosts mapped in the last 7 days.

          • host/bounty recon map --all

            Map every host again, recent ones too.

          • e

            In look here, after a map: the 13 exposure checks on live hosts. A hit adds the exposed chip and raises interest.

          • ↵y

            In look here: ↵ shows the host’s URL, y copies it.

        5. 4Test by hand

          • b

            Open Burp through the guard. Paste the URL into Burp’s browser (Proxy, then Open browser).

          • R

            Site broken? The newest requests the guard refused. Approve the program’s CDN or login hosts for your own browsing only (GET, HEAD, OPTIONS). Tools, maps, replays and Claude stay refused.

          • host/bounty program browse

            Hosts your browsing hit that aren’t in scope, and the ones you approved.

          • host/bounty program browse allow <host>…

            Add --any-method for a login host that needs POST.

          • t

            A shell in the guard container. Tools are held to scope and the program’s rate.

          • h

            The Claude copilot. Reads recon and access results and drafts findings. It runs only the checks you approve, one at a time, through the guard.

        6. 5Two accounts

          • Log in as account A in Burp’s browser, and as B in its incognito window (⌘⇧N). Don’t log out of either.

          • v

            Open the access check.

          • 12

            1 pastes A’s Cookie (and X-Csrf-Token) lines, ⌘↵ saves. 2 does the same for B.

          • Click around the site as A.

          • Space↵

            In v: Space picks requests, ! allows one that changes data, ↵ replays them as B and with no session.

          • Tab

            Results. Confirm every CHECK by hand in Burp before reporting.

        7. 6Write the finding

          • fn

            New draft at workspace/bounty/findings/<handle>-<slug>/finding.md; ↵ creates it and opens it in your editor. h can draft one too.

          • Fill in summary, steps, impact, severity or CVSS, and asset.

          • host/bounty ac candidates

            Flow IDs of A’s requests. host/bounty flows --json has every flow’s flow_id.

          • host/bounty finding evidence <id> <flow-id>…

            Attach flows with credentials redacted. Bodies are kept: review them first.

          • host/bounty flows export <handle> <dir>

            The full flow dump, to a folder on this Mac.

        8. 7Submit

          • Paste finding.md into Intigriti’s form and upload evidence/. Submitting is manual.

          • Set status: in the front matter by hand, and payout_eur: once it pays.

          • f

            Hours and €/h. A program’s €/h uses payout_eur; the all-programs total uses your Intigriti payouts, which arrive with the next sync.

          • host/bounty time report

            Hours, findings, payouts and €/h per program.
